What is Jianpu?

Understanding numbered notation and its relationship with traditional music reading. A comprehensive guide for students and educators.

The Universal Language of Numbers

Jianpu (简谱), which translates directly to "simplified notation," is the widely recognized Chinese term for numbered musical notation. Unlike the western five-line staff, Jianpu utilizes the numbers 1 through 7 to represent the notes of the major scale (Do, Re, Mi, Fa, Sol, La, Ti).

Developed to be accessible and intuitive, it is the primary method of musical instruction in many Eastern educational systems. Its simplicity allows beginners to quickly grasp melodic structures without the immediate cognitive load of deciphering complex staff symbols, making it an invaluable tool for ear training and initial musical literacy.
