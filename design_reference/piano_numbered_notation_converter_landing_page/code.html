How Piano Players Use Numbered Notation

Numbered notation, also known as Jianpu, is a popular way to quickly jot down melodies. For pianists, it represents the degrees of the scale, making it easier to transpose and understand melodic structure before moving to standard staff notation.

Piano Notation FAQ

Can piano numbered notation become sheet music?

expand_more

Yes, with NotaBridge, you can instantly transform any numbered sequence into standard Western staff notation ready for printing or practice.

How do I read piano numbers?

expand_more

In the key of C Major, 1 corresponds to C, 2 to D, and so on. Piano numbers represent the scale degrees of your current key.
